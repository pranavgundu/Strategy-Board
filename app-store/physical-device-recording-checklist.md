# Physical-device recording checklist

Apple requires this recording to be made on a physical iPhone or iPad running the latest operating system. A Simulator recording is not acceptable.

1. Install build 26.6.1 (public version 26.6.0) from TestFlight on the physical device.
2. Turn on Do Not Disturb and use landscape orientation.
3. Start the built-in screen recorder in Control Center.
4. Launch Strategy Board from the Home Screen so the recording includes app launch.
5. Create a match named `Qualification 12` with red teams `254`, `1678`, `4414` and blue teams `2056`, `1323`, `973`.
6. Open the match and draw a short path in Auto.
7. Briefly visit Transition, Active, Inactive/Endgame, Notes, and Stats.
8. Return to the match list.
9. Open TBA import briefly to show the optional public-data workflow, then cancel.
10. Open the Share or QR export controls briefly. Do not show personal notifications or credentials.
11. Stop recording. Keep it concise (about 60–120 seconds) and do not edit it in a way that hides the physical-device status bar or launch sequence.
12. AirDrop the `.mov` file to this Mac and place it in `app-store/review-recording/`.
